# FAR_NZY — SYSTEM BUILD PLAIN

## Objective

Create a visually immersive underground techno-alchemical ecosystem that functions as:
- a social simulation game
- a vault management simulator
- a crystal cultivation economy
- a future tokenized treasury framework
- a deterministic and auditable entertainment economy

The game front-end is the abstraction layer for the underlying economy.

The system should appear:
- alive
- lawful
- ancient
- technological
- mysterious
- economically trustworthy

---

# CORE SYSTEMS

## 1. BIO GARDENS

### Purpose
Primary free-to-play faucet and emotional retention loop.

### Gameplay
Players:
- cultivate biological ecosystems
- harvest spores and plants
- generate FarqlDust (FD)
- unlock mutations
- automate growth cycles

### Economy Role
Responsible for:
- onboarding
- casual engagement
- soft currency generation
- player recovery stabilization

### Design Rule
BIO GARDENS must remain viable for non-paying users.

Never allow staking systems to invalidate this gameplay path.

---

## 2. VAULT SIM

### Purpose
Infrastructure and governance layer.

### Gameplay
Players construct:
- underground vaults
- server rooms
- crystal reactors
- AI chambers
- treasury reserves
- automated systems

### Economy Role
Vaults represent:
- trust
- treasury health
- infrastructure prestige
- staking authority
- automation scaling

### Design Rule
The vault is the visual representation of economic legitimacy.

---

## 3. CRYSTAL ALCHEMY

### Purpose
Long-term retention and staking abstraction system.

### Gameplay
Players:
- cultivate crystals
- stabilize resonance
- mine rare materials
- refine geological energy
- stake PDX through crystal growth

### Economy Role
Crystal systems abstract:
- treasury locking
- staking
- reserve stabilization
- delayed emissions
- long-duration participation

### Important Crystal
VOIDSHARD:
- near-black ultraviolet crystal
- unstable geometry
- highest prestige tier
- represents long-duration staking confidence

---

# CURRENCY SYSTEMS

## FD — FarqlDust

### Role
Soft utility currency.

### Uses
- crafting
- upgrades
- cosmetics
- farming
- repairs
- ecosystem expansion

### Rules
- high circulation
- broad accessibility
- sustainable generation
- inflation controlled through sinks

---

## SC — SweepCoins

### Role
Sweepstakes/social participation layer.

### Uses
- event entries
- social rain systems
- ceremonial reward systems

### Rules
- transparent participation
- visible entry systems
- legally reviewable architecture
- no deceptive mechanics

---

## PDX — PrimeDie

### Role
Governance and reserve asset.

### Uses
- staking
- treasury participation
- governance systems
- prestige access
- reserve stability

### Long-Term Vision
PDX may evolve into a cryptographic asset.

The architecture must therefore:
- remain modular
- remain auditable
- remain legally adaptable
- separate economy logic from presentation logic

---

# STAKING ABSTRACTION SYSTEM

## Concept
Traditional staking systems are replaced visually with crystal growth.

Instead of:
- APY dashboards
- finance UIs
- token lock charts

Players interact with:
- resonance chambers
- geological growth cycles
- underground crystal vaults

---

# Example Flow

Player:
- stakes 100 PDX
- for 365 days

System Response:
- VOIDSHARD seed implanted
- crystal grows over time
- vault visuals evolve
- resonance energy intensifies
- social prestige increases

At maturity:
- principal returned
- yield distributed
- crystal harvested
- cosmetic rewards unlocked

---

# BALANCE PHILOSOPHY

## Primary Goal
The economy must survive long-term.

Not maximize short-term extraction.

---

## Mandatory Rules

### No Hidden Manipulation
All probabilities must be:
- internally auditable
- version tracked
- governance reviewable

### Recovery Systems Required
Players must have:
- comeback paths
- frustration stabilization
- retention recovery systems
- morale recovery events

### Anti-Whale Measures
Prevent:
- economic domination
- staking monopolies
- infinite extraction cycles

### Treasury Stability
Treasury systems exist to:
- stabilize ecosystem health
- prevent collapse
- reinforce trust

---

Cosmetics represent:
- participation age
- vault status

---

# IMPORTANT ENGINEERING PRINCIPLES

## The Game Is The Interface

Do NOT design the economy like:
- a casino backend with game visuals attached

Instead:
- the world itself IS the economy visualization layer

Everything symbolic:
- crystals = reserves
- vaults = trust
- resonance = staking
- biological growth = emissions
- corruption = instability
