## What the corpus FALSIFIES about King of Tokyo
- **"Escalation is a distinct depth primitive" — DESTROYED [SI, decisive].** The audit shows escalation is
  *rising-stakes = rising-variance-under-commitment*, "which the hot-dice multiplier ladder ALREADY
  instantiates. Escalation is a PROPERTY of the commitment loop, not a new variable." Counter-quadrants
  are full (slot machines = high escalation / zero depth; Go = deep / flat stakes).
  → **Implication for us:** *Hold the Crown*'s growing hold-multiplier is the correct, honest instantiation
  of this property. We must NOT market "escalation" as novel depth — it is the commitment loop under rising
  variance, nothing more.
- **King of Tokyo needs no "transformation" primitive [SI].** It decomposes cleanly into
  **commitment (stay/flee) + calibration (survival odds) + intervention (attack/heal) + consequence.**

## What the corpus DISCOVERS as King of Tokyo's distinctive experience
- **King of Tokyo = "push-luck + SPOTLIGHT" [SI].** Physically, "the Tokyo occupant is the table's focus;
  everyone reacts to their yield/stay." What is **LOST digitally** is the shared physical spotlight/goading;
  what is **GAINED** is automation + solo-vs-AI; the **UNEXPLORED** frontier is *"an AUDIENCE around the
  spotlight (arena layered on the table)"* — witnessed-risk at scale (UT3, "the strongest social phenomenon
  from prior research").
- **Cluster placement [SI]:** CLUSTER-PRESSURE (with Quacks + Farkle): *compulsion = own exposure /
  push-or-stop; foresight = probabilistic; luck = core tension.* Its question is *"can I hold?"*
- **Environment mediates experience [SI]:** the same competency in a *contested* Tokyo yields a different
  experience — environment is the mediating variable between competency and experience.

## Design consequences for our ecosystem
1. **Hold the Crown (commitment/contestability) is on solid, verified ground.** commitment loop + rising-
   variance multiplier + bust-consequence = the falsification-surviving core. Keep it; claim nothing more.
2. **Add the missing CALIBRATION surface [SI → build].** The corpus names calibration (survival odds) as a
   KoT component. We surface a per-round **bust-risk read** so the hold decision is a *calibrated* wager, not
   a blind one. Note: per-round bust risk is **constant** (each round is 6 fresh fair dice) — the *stakes*
   rise, not the per-round odds. That is precisely "rising-variance-under-commitment," shown honestly. We
   surface the read qualitatively (informing, not solving — "meaningful, not maximal").
3. **The distinctive SPOTLIGHT/witnessed-risk essence is a GATED branch [SP → route to a gate].** True
   audience/spotlight is inherently multiplayer + spectator, and *spectator information asymmetry tensions
   with symmetric fairness*. So the `commitment/social-witness` branch stays **planned/dormant** until:
   - multiplayer/spectator infrastructure exists (deploy/real-users → **G2**), and
   - if it ever carries real stakes, counsel + value-model → **G1/G4**.
   In closed-loop solo we may offer only a **single-player "on-stage" framing** (a shareable *witnessed
   record* of a run — self-as-audience), never real multi-human spectating. Nothing here crosses a gate.
